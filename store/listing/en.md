## Description

Converto converts your files directly on your phone. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is tracked.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Choose a file. The format is detected automatically.
2. Pick the format you want. Only conversions that make sense are offered.
3. Adjust the options if you wish, then convert.
4. Save, share or open the result.

WHAT YOU CAN CONVERT
• Images: JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, AVIF, GIF, BMP, TIFF, and image to PDF
• PDF: to images (one per page or a single long image), to text, merge, extract pages, rotate
• Documents: Markdown, HTML, text and DOCX to PDF or text
• Data: CSV, JSON and XLSX in any direction
• Audio: MP3, AAC, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG, Opus, AIFF
• Video: MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, animated GIF, or extract the audio
• Archives: create and extract ZIP

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works fully offline
• No ads, no account, no tracking, no cookies
• Free, with no in-app purchases
• Your files never leave your device

ACCESSIBLE
Screen reader support, adjustable text size, light and dark themes. Available in English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese and Dutch.
